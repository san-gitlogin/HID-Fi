# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. This is a small hobby project and nobody is paid to be here.

## Expected

- Assume good faith. Most confusion is a wrong USB port or a charge-only cable,
  not incompetence.
- Be specific in issues. "It does not work" costs everyone time; the board, the
  ports, and the output of `tests/test_v34_features.py` cost you one minute and
  save an hour.
- Accept that answers are sometimes "no". Some requests are impossible because HID
  is one way, and no amount of code changes that.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, personal attacks, or discrimination of any kind.
- Demanding work from unpaid maintainers or contributors.
- Publishing others' private information.
- Using this project to do something to a computer you do not own or have
  permission to control. This is a keystroke injection device. Do not be the
  reason it gets a bad name.

## Enforcement

Report anything to `san.phplogin@gmail.com`. I will look into it and respond as
fairly as I can. I may remove comments, close issues, or block accounts.

I am one person, not a committee. I will act in good faith and I will not always
get it right.
