HID-Fi Core - print notes (generated by build.mjs)

All files in this folder are already turned the way they sit on the bed. Do not rotate them.
Material: PETG (PLA only for fit tests). 0.4 mm nozzle. No supports for any part.
Every mating gap is 0.3 mm, so a coarse print (0.2-0.28 mm layers, visible lines) still closes.

Print in this order:
  1. kit-test.stl    - fit-coupon + latch-coupon + plate, about 7 g. Slide the board's sockets into the
                       fit coupon and try your thickest cable. Hook the plate's front tabs into the fit
                       coupon's slots, then clip its back into the latch coupon: it should click.
  2. unibody.stl     - 0.12-0.2 mm layers, 5 walls, top face down (as supplied), elephant-foot
                       compensation 0.15 mm, cooling 100% after the first layers, seam at rear.
  3. plate.stl       - 0.2 mm layers, as supplied (flat side and lettering down). plate-feet.stl instead
                       if you want silicone feet (optional). The plate from step 1 is a real plate.
  The status lights need no part: power (red), and the blue and green data lights, shine through a slim rounded light bar in the lid, each in its own segment.
  trim-guide.stl     - only if your board came with header pins soldered.

Assembly: board into the unibody (sockets first, slide forward). Hook the plate's two front tabs into
the slots behind the ports, swing the back up and press: the two catches click into the window in
the back wall. To open: push both catches in through that window with a fingernail, put a coin in
the notch under it and twist; the back drops out, then lift the tabs out of their slots.
Nothing is a press fit and nothing should need force. If it does, do not force it: tell us what you
measured (issue tracker) and print the coupons again with the fix.
