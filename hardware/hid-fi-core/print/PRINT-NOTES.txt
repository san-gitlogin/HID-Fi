HID-Fi Core - print notes (generated by build.mjs)

All files in this folder are already turned the way they sit on the bed. Do not rotate them.
Material: PETG (PLA only for fit tests). 0.4 mm nozzle. No supports for any part.

Print in this order:
  1. fit-coupon.stl  - 0.12 mm layers. Check the board sockets and your thickest cable fit.
  2. peg-gauge.stl   - 0.2 mm layers. Press pegs 1, 2, 3 into holes 1, 2, 3. Pick the one that is
                       snug but comes out with a fingernail. 2 is the default (PEG.fit 0.08 mm).
  3. unibody.stl     - 0.12 mm layers, 5 walls, top face down (as supplied), textured sheet if you have one,
                       elephant-foot compensation 0.15 mm, cooling 100% after the first layers, seam at rear.
  4. plate.stl       - 0.2 mm layers, as supplied (flat side and lettering down).
                       plate-feet.stl instead if you want silicone feet (optional).
  5. light-pipe.stl  - clear or natural PETG, 0.08 mm, flange down. Optional: clear hot glue works too.
  trim-guide.stl     - only if your board came with header pins soldered.

Assembly: board into the unibody (sockets first, slide forward), hook the plate front tongue into the
front slot, press the back down until flush: the two pegs click into the posts. To open: a coin in the
notch at the back edge of the plate, lever gently.
